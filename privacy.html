Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Longevity Life Academy ("LLA", "we", "us" or "our") is a brand operated by eTeacher Ltd., an Israeli company located at 8 Ahliav Street, Ramat Gan, Israel 5252261.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you use the Longevity Life Academy mobile application and related account, program, support and wellness services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the "Services").

The Services are intended only for individuals aged 18 years or older.

LLA is an educational and wellness service. It is not a medical or healthcare service and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or medical condition. Information, scores, recommendations and other content provided through the Services are for educational and general wellness purposes and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

1.1 Account and Identity Information

We may collect:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • age or date of birth;
  • sex;
  • account and authentication identifiers;
  • Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in identifiers;
  • communication preferences; and
  • information you provide when contacting us.

The App uses email-based authentication and may use Apple or Google sign-in services.

1.2 Health and Wellness Data

The Services allow you to provide, and in some cases authorize us to receive, information relating to your health, fitness, lifestyle and wellbeing ("Health and Wellness Data").

This may include:

  • age and sex;
  • height and weight;
  • sleep duration, stages, efficiency, latency and related sleep information;
  • heart rate and resting heart rate;
  • heart-rate variability (HRV);
  • steps, workouts, active energy, active minutes and exercise information;
  • VO₂ max;
  • blood pressure;
  • blood oxygen;
  • respiratory rate;
  • glucose and continuous glucose monitoring information;
  • mindfulness activity;
  • nutrition, meals and hydration;
  • mood and stress information;
  • supplements;
  • health and wellness goals;
  • symptoms or health concerns that you choose to provide;
  • medical conditions or medical history that you choose to provide;
  • medications;
  • laboratory and blood-test results, including information such as fasting glucose, HbA1c and lipid results;
  • sleep notes;
  • completion of daily wellness practices; and
  • other health or wellness information that you choose to enter into the Services.

Health and Wellness Data is sensitive information. We use it only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell Health and Wellness Data. We do not use Health and Wellness Data for advertising, targeted advertising or marketing, and we do not provide it to data brokers or advertising networks.

1.3 Apple Health Data

If you use the App on iOS and grant permission, the App may read information from Apple Health.

Depending on the information available in your Apple Health account and the permissions you grant, the App may read:

  • Step Count
  • Heart Rate
  • Resting Heart Rate
  • HRV (SDNN)
  • Sleep Analysis
  • Active Energy Burned
  • Mindful Session
  • Workout
  • Blood Glucose
  • Blood Pressure Systolic
  • Blood Pressure Diastolic
  • VO₂ Max
  • Water
  • Exercise Time
  • Respiratory Rate
  • Oxygen Saturation

The App may synchronize permitted Apple Health information in the background.

Access is read-only. LLA does not write information to or modify information in Apple Health.

Height and weight are not read from Apple Health and are collected only when you provide them directly to LLA.

LLA does not use Apple Health information for advertising or marketing and does not sell or disclose Apple Health information to data brokers.

The LLA App does not use iCloud or CloudKit to store or synchronize the health information it collects. Your use of Apple Health itself remains subject to Apple's own services, settings and privacy practices.

You control which Apple Health information LLA may access through Apple's health and privacy permission settings and may revoke those permissions at any time.

1.4 Health Connect Data

If you use the App on Android and grant permission, the App may read information from Health Connect while the App is open.

Depending on the information available and the permissions you grant, LLA currently reads:

  • steps;
  • heart rate;
  • resting heart rate;
  • heart-rate variability (HRV);
  • sleep information;
  • active calories;
  • exercise sessions; and
  • mindfulness activity.

Access is read-only. LLA does not write information back to Health Connect.

Height and weight are not read from Health Connect and are collected only when you provide them directly to LLA.

You can manage or revoke LLA's Health Connect permissions through Health Connect or the applicable Android privacy settings.

Revoking Apple Health or Health Connect permissions stops future access from that source but does not automatically delete information that LLA previously received. You may delete previously collected information by deleting your account or exercising your applicable privacy rights as described below.

1.5 Wearables and Connected Devices

LLA currently has no direct integration with manufacturers of smart watches, smart rings or other wearable devices.

However, a wearable or another application may independently write information to Apple Health or Health Connect. If you have authorized LLA to read the applicable information from Apple Health or Health Connect, information originally generated by such a device may therefore reach LLA indirectly through those platforms.

Where available, we may retain information identifying the source from which a health measurement was obtained.

1.6 Manually Entered Information

You may also enter information directly into the App, including health measurements, laboratory results, CGM averages, mood, stress, meals, hydration, supplements, sleep notes and completed wellness practices.

Information that you enter manually is treated as Health and Wellness Data under this Privacy Policy.

1.7 Longevity Scores and Derived Information

LLA uses Health and Wellness Data to calculate and maintain information such as:

  • your Longevity Score;
  • your Longevity Age;
  • six individual wellness-pillar scores;
  • trends and changes in relevant measurements;
  • completion of daily practices; and
  • personalized wellness information and recommendations.

The App may maintain a daily record or "snapshot" containing health measurements available for that day, manually entered information, the source of the information where available, calculated scores and completed practices.

Both underlying measurements and calculated or derived results may be stored on our systems.

1.8 Analytics and Technical Information

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve its operation.

Information sent to Firebase Analytics may include:

  • Firebase app-instance identifier;
  • LLA user identifier;
  • App version;
  • operating system;
  • screen and usage events; and
  • an age-group range, such as 40–49.

We do not send the user's exact age or health measurements to Firebase Analytics.

The App is designed so that Health and Wellness Data is excluded from analytics events through technical controls.

We do not currently use Firebase Crashlytics or another crash-reporting SDK in the App.

1.9 Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, we collect and process a device notification token so that we can send App notifications and reminders.

We use Expo's push-notification service to relay notification tokens.

Push notifications are designed not to contain your Longevity Score or individual health measurements. Notifications may, for example, tell you that you have practices remaining for the day, provide information about your activity streak, or provide a general reminder.

You can disable notifications through your device settings and, where available, through the App.

1.10 Communications With LLA

If you communicate with LLA, we may process the information contained in your communications and information necessary to respond to your request.

The App does not provide an instructor or administrative portal through which instructors can access your App health records.

You may nevertheless choose separately to share information with an LLA instructor or other LLA representative. For example, you may voluntarily share information derived from a glucose-monitoring device. Information that you voluntarily provide in that manner will be treated in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Lingo Continuous Glucose Monitor

LLA may provide or resell access to the Lingo continuous glucose monitoring product operated by Abbott as part of an LLA program.

Lingo is a separate third-party product and service operated by Abbott or its applicable affiliates.

LLA does not currently have a technical integration with Lingo and does not automatically receive your continuous glucose readings from Abbott or the Lingo application.

Your use of Lingo, including information collected directly through the Lingo device or application, is subject to Abbott/Lingo's own terms and privacy practices.

You may choose to:

  • manually enter information derived from Lingo into LLA; or
  • voluntarily share Lingo-derived information with an LLA instructor or representative.

Once you provide that information to LLA, we treat it as Health and Wellness Data under this Privacy Policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and as required or permitted by applicable law.

Active Accounts

Health and Wellness Data, daily snapshots, scores and account information may be retained while your account remains active so that we can provide the Services and maintain your wellness history.

Account Deletion

The App includes a Delete Account function in the Profile section.

When you delete your account, your user account and associated data in LLA's active application systems are scheduled for immediate deletion. LLA does not intentionally maintain a soft-deleted copy of your health profile in its active application database.

Where personal information associated with your account is maintained by a service provider, we will cause deletion or de-identification to be completed within 30 days following the applicable account-deletion request, except where retention is legally required or permitted.

Residual copies contained in backups may remain for up to 90 days before being overwritten or deleted in accordance with our backup-management procedures.

Information that we are legally required to retain, or that is reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, may be retained for the applicable required period and will not be used for other purposes.

Analytics

User-associated analytics information is retained only for legitimate App operation and improvement purposes and is subject to our configured analytics-retention practices.

Following account deletion, user-associated analytics information is subject to the deletion/de-identification process described above.
